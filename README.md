## The mark

Two rules hold the top and bottom of a Z. The diagonal is never drawn: two nodes sit on it, at one third and two thirds of its length. Built on a 64 x 64 unit grid.

| Variant | Use |
|---------|-----|
| Primary cut | Rule stroke 9, nodes r 5.2 — 32 px and up |
| Small cut | Rule stroke 10, nodes r 6 — 16 to 31 px |
| Contained | Knocked out of a solid tile — app icons, favicon |

- **Clear space:** 2x on every side, where x is the rule width
- **Minimum:** mark 24 px · lock-up 104 px wide · print 12 mm wide
- **Wordmark:** Space Grotesk Medium 500, lowercase, -0.055em tracking

## Color

| Name | Hex | RGB | Use |
|------|-----|-----|-----|
| Cobalt Ink | `#10214F` | 16 33 79 | Primary dark |
| Paper | `#F4F5F7` | 244 245 247 | Light background |
| Live Cobalt | `#2050C4` | 32 80 196 | Accent, links |
| Near-black | `#101012` | 16 16 18 | UI dark |

Support: Slate `#4A5468` · Grey `#6E7280` · Hairline `#C7D2EA` · Wash `#E4E9F4`

Proportion: 58% paper, 28% ink, 9% live cobalt, 5% wash. No third brand color and no gradient.

## Typography

- **Space Grotesk** (300 / 400 / 500 / 700) — wordmark, headings, UI, body
- **JetBrains Mono** (400 / 500) — addresses, hashes, amounts, chain IDs

Both are open source (SIL OFL) on [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/).

## Do not

- Stretch, squash, or rotate the mark
- Add a diagonal, outline, shadow, or glow
- Apply gradients or colors outside the palette
- Set the wordmark in another face, capitals, or different tracking
